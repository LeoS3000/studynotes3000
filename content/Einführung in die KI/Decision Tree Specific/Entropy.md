$H(Y)$ of a random variable $Y$ is given by $$H(Y)=-\sum_{r=1}^R{P(Y=y_r)log_2P(Y=y_r)}$$
- More uncertainty -> more entropy
- In infomation theory:
	- $H(Y)$ is the expected number of bits needed to encode a randomly drawn value of $Y$ 
		- under most efficient code
